SAPS police clearance for New Zealand residence & visa applications

How Immigration New Zealand's character and police certificate requirements apply to South African citizens — and where a SAPS certificate fits in.

The General Picture

Immigration New Zealand's police certificate requirement

Immigration New Zealand (INZ) assesses character as part of most residence-class visa applications — Skilled Migrant Category, Green List, Partnership Residence and others. As part of that assessment, INZ generally requires a police certificate from every country you hold citizenship of, regardless of how long you've actually lived there, plus every country you've lived in for 12 months or more (cumulatively) since turning 17. For South African citizens, that means a SAPS Police Clearance Certificate is very often required — including for people who left South Africa years ago, simply because citizenship alone can trigger the requirement.

INZ's published guidance for residence applications also asks for the certificate to be less than 6 months old at the time you submit your application — worth knowing early, since SAPS processing and fingerprint logistics from New Zealand take real lead time (see How Long It Takes).

We are not a licensed immigration adviser and don't complete or advise on your INZ application; for the New Zealand-side requirements, always confirm directly with immigration.govt.nz or a licensed immigration adviser.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need a SAPS police clearance for a New Zealand resident visa?

+

For most residence-class visas — including Skilled Migrant, Green List and Partnership Residence — Immigration New Zealand requires a police certificate from every country you hold citizenship of, regardless of how long you've lived there, plus every country you've lived in for 12 months or more since turning 17. As a South African citizen, that generally means a SAPS Police Clearance Certificate is required documentation. Always confirm the exact requirement for your specific visa category on immigration.govt.nz, since policy can change.

How recent does my SAPS certificate need to be for INZ?

+

Immigration New Zealand's published guidance for residence visa applications asks for a police certificate less than 6 months old at the time the application is submitted. Check the current requirement listed for your specific visa category on immigration.govt.nz before relying on this.

Does a temporary visa (like a work or visitor visa) need one too?

+

The clearest, most consistent requirement is for residence-class visas. Some temporary visa categories may ask for one depending on your circumstances — the official requirement for your specific visa type is set out on immigration.govt.nz, so check there rather than assuming.

Can I get my SAPS certificate sorted while I'm already living in New Zealand?

+

Yes — that's exactly what EM Services coordinates from Christchurch: fingerprint capture, documentation review, and submission to SAPS, for South Africans already based anywhere in New Zealand.

Where do I find the official, current INZ requirement?

+

Immigration New Zealand's own guidance (immigration.govt.nz) is the authoritative source — police certificate rules are set and updated centrally by INZ and can change, so always check there for your specific visa category rather than relying on general guidance like this page.
