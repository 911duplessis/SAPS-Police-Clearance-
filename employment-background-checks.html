SAPS police clearance for employment background checks

Not every SAPS Police Clearance Certificate is for a visa — here's how it works when a New Zealand employer or background check asks for one.

The General Picture

When employment, not immigration, is the reason

Most people who need a SAPS Police Clearance Certificate are applying for a visa somewhere — but not all. Some New Zealand employers and recruiters run background checks that cover every country a candidate has lived in, particularly for roles involving finance, vulnerable people, security clearance, or other positions of trust. If South Africa is one of those countries for you, the employer may specifically ask for a SAPS certificate rather than accepting a New Zealand-only check.

The good news: the underlying process is identical to any other SAPS application — fingerprints captured to SAPS standard, documentation reviewed, and the application submitted and tracked through to the finished certificate. The only thing that changes is who's asking for it and what they do with it once it arrives.

Our role: EM Services coordinates the SAPS Police Clearance Certificate itself. We don't set or interpret any specific employer's background-check policy — confirm exactly what's required, and in what format, directly with your employer or recruiter.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Why would an employer ask for a SAPS police clearance?

+

Some New Zealand employers — particularly for roles involving finance, vulnerable people, security, or other positions of trust — ask candidates for a background check covering every country they've lived in, not just New Zealand. If you've lived in South Africa, that can mean a SAPS Police Clearance Certificate specifically. Whether it applies to you depends entirely on the employer or role — check with your employer or recruiter about their specific screening requirements.

Is the process different from an immigration SAPS certificate?

+

No — it's the same fingerprint capture, documentation review, and SAPS submission process regardless of why you need it. Only the stated purpose on the application, and who ultimately receives the finished certificate, differs.

How long does an employment-purpose SAPS certificate take?

+

The same realistic timeframe as any SAPS Police Clearance Certificate — see How Long It Takes for the current estimate. It's worth starting the process as soon as you know a background check is coming, since SAPS processing time isn't something we can accelerate.

Do I need a certificate from every country I've worked in, or just South Africa?

+

That's entirely up to the employer's own background-check policy — some ask only about your country of citizenship, others ask about every country of residence over a set period. Confirm the specific scope with your employer or recruiter before assuming what's needed.

Can EM Services address the certificate directly to my employer?

+

SAPS Police Clearance Certificates are issued to the applicant. Check with your employer how they'd like you to provide it — original document, certified copy, or another format — and let us know if there's anything specific about how it needs to be presented.
