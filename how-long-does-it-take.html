Timelines, explained

How long does SA police clearance actually take from NZ?

Short answer: 6–10 weeks from submission. Here's what happens in that window, stage by stage, so you're not just told a number — you know what it means.

1. Fingerprint capture & document prep — Christchurch

+

Elanza captures your fingerprints personally and prepares your SAPS application, cover letter and supporting documents. This is the one part entirely within your control — typically booked and completed within days, not weeks.

2. Courier to South Africa

+

SAPS doesn't accept electronic applications — your file has to physically travel. Budget roughly a week for the outbound courier leg.

3. SAPS internal processing — Pretoria

+

This is the bulk of the wait, and the part no provider anywhere — in NZ, Australia, or South Africa — can control or expedite. It's the South African Police Service's own Criminal Record Centre workload.

4. Courier back to New Zealand

+

Once SAPS issues your clearance certificate, it's couriered back — another week, roughly. Elanza receives and forwards it to you the same day it lands.

Have an urgent deadline?

+

WhatsApp Elanza with your specific date. Priority scheduling and same-week submission can tighten the NZ-side steps — she'll tell you plainly what's realistic rather than over-promising on the part she doesn't control.
