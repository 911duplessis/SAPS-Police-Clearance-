SAPS police clearance for a UK visa

How a South African criminal record check fits into UK Home Office visa and settlement requirements.

The General Picture

When the UK asks for an overseas criminal record check

The UK Home Office requires an overseas criminal record certificate for certain visa categories — most notably some Skilled Worker roles in regulated sectors such as education, healthcare and social care — as well as for some settlement (Indefinite Leave to Remain) and British citizenship applications. It is not a blanket requirement for every UK visa, so the first step is always confirming whether your specific visa category asks for one, using the official gov.uk guidance for that route.

Where it is required, applicants who have lived in South Africa are expected to provide a police clearance covering that period — which is where a SAPS Police Clearance Certificate comes in. Because Home Office document requirements (validity windows, certified translation rules, submission format) are set centrally and updated periodically, this page is a starting orientation, not a substitute for checking your specific route's current requirements.

Our role: EM Services is based in Christchurch, New Zealand, and coordinates SAPS Police Clearance Certificates for South Africans applying from New Zealand. We are not a UK immigration adviser and don't complete UK visa applications — for the UK-side requirements, always confirm directly with gov.uk or a licensed UK immigration adviser.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Does every UK visa require a South African police clearance?

+

No — it depends on the visa category. The UK generally requires an overseas criminal record certificate for certain Skilled Worker roles (particularly in education, healthcare and social care) and for some settlement and nationality applications, rather than for every visa type. Always check the specific requirement listed for your visa category on the official UK government (gov.uk) guidance, since requirements are set and updated by the Home Office.

How recent does the certificate need to be for a UK application?

+

The UK Home Office typically specifies a validity window (commonly documents no older than a set number of months at the point of application or decision) — check the current guidance for your specific visa category on gov.uk, since this detail is set centrally and can change.

Does the certificate need to be translated for a UK application?

+

SAPS certificates are issued in English, so translation generally isn't the issue it can be for non-English-speaking source countries. Confirm against your specific visa category's document requirements regardless.

Can I apply for my SAPS certificate from the UK if I've already emigrated?

+

Yes — fingerprints for a SAPS application can be captured wherever you currently live, including the UK, by a suitably qualified provider, and your file couriered to Pretoria. If you're applying from New Zealand instead, that's exactly the service Elanza provides in Christchurch.

Where can I find the official, current UK requirement?

+

Always check gov.uk directly for your specific visa route — Skilled Worker, Health and Care Worker, settlement, or citizenship requirements each have their own criminal record check rules, and official government guidance is the only source that stays current.
