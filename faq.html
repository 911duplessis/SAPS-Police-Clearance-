SAPS Police Clearance — Frequently Asked Questions

Every common question, organised by topic, answered in plain language. Can't find yours? WhatsApp Elanza directly.

The Certificate Itself

What is a SAPS Police Clearance Certificate?

+

An official document issued by the South African Police Service's Criminal Record Centre confirming whether you have a criminal record in South Africa, based on your fingerprints. See our full explainer.

Does one certificate cover every province I've lived in?

+

Yes. SAPS runs a single, centralised database, so one certificate covers your full South African record regardless of how many provinces you lived in.

How long is a SAPS certificate valid for?

+

SAPS doesn't print an expiry date — the receiving authority decides how recent they need it, and six to twelve months old is the most common requirement. Always confirm with whoever is requesting it.

Applying From New Zealand

Do I need to travel to South Africa to apply?

+

No. Fingerprints are captured wherever you currently live — in Elanza's case, in person in Christchurch — and your file is couriered to Pretoria for processing.

How long does it take from New Zealand?

+

Standard processing is 6–10 weeks from SAPS submission. See our full timeline breakdown.

Is Elanza's service available outside Christchurch?

+

Yes — while fingerprint capture happens in person in Christchurch, document coordination is available to South Africans across New Zealand.

Documents & Fingerprints

What documents do I need?

+

Typically your South African ID or passport, a completed SAPS application form, certified fingerprints, and a cover letter. See the full checklist.

Why do fingerprints matter so much?

+

They're the actual mechanism SAPS uses to match you to the national database — poor-quality prints are the leading cause of applications being sent back. See our fingerprint guide.

Does Elanza capture fingerprints herself?

+

Yes — she's a certified fingerprint technician, certified by Forensic Insight Ltd and a partner of Fingerprint Services NZ, and captures every client's prints personally.

Cost & Apostille

How much does it cost?

+

It's made up of several separate line items — SAPS fees, courier, optional apostille, and service coordination — rather than one fixed number. See our full cost breakdown, or WhatsApp for a transparent quote.

Do I need my certificate apostilled?

+

Only if the receiving authority specifically requires it — not every use case does. See our apostille guide to check.

Using It Abroad

Which countries accept a SAPS Police Clearance Certificate?

+

It's accepted for NZ residency and immigration processes in Australia, the UK, Canada, the UAE, Singapore and most OECD countries — the exact requirement depends on the receiving authority's own rules.

Do requirements differ by destination country?

+

Yes — see our destination-specific guidance for the UK and Australia.

Deadlines & Problems

What if I have an urgent deadline?

+

WhatsApp Elanza with your specific date. Priority scheduling and fast document preparation can tighten the New Zealand side of the process — but SAPS's own internal processing time can't be sped up by anyone.

What are the most common causes of delay?

+

Poor fingerprint quality, inconsistent document details, and not checking apostille requirements upfront. See our full mistakes guide.

I've lost my certificate — can it be reissued from a copy?

+

No — a new application has to be submitted from scratch, including new fingerprints. There's no shortcut for a lost certificate, so keep the original safe once it arrives.
