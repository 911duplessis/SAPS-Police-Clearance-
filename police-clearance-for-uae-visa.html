SAPS police clearance for a UAE visa

How UAE residency and employment visa applications treat South African police clearance certificates, and what attestation typically involves.

The General Picture

UAE police certificate & attestation requirements

Most UAE residency visas — whether arranged through an employer, a Golden Visa route, or family sponsorship — are processed through the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Security (ICP) or a specific Emirate's General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA). Many of these routes ask for a police clearance certificate from countries an applicant has lived in, which for most South African applicants means a SAPS Police Clearance Certificate.

The UAE typically expects a foreign police certificate to be legalised before it will be accepted — usually an apostille (South Africa is a member of the Hague Apostille Convention) followed by attestation through the UAE embassy or consulate, and sometimes a further step with the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs once you're in-country. This attestation chain, and the exact validity window required, is set by the sponsor and the relevant UAE authority and can change, so always confirm current requirements before relying on general guidance like this page.

Our role: EM Services coordinates SAPS Police Clearance Certificates for South Africans based in New Zealand — including those applying for UAE residency or employment visas. We are not a UAE visa consultant and don't handle UAE-side attestation directly; for the UAE-specific requirements and attestation process, confirm with your sponsor, employer, or a licensed UAE visa specialist.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Does the UAE require a police clearance certificate from South Africa?

+

Many UAE residency and employment visa routes — arranged through ICP or a specific Emirate's GDRFA — ask for a police clearance certificate (sometimes called a "good conduct certificate") from countries you've lived in, which for most South African applicants means a SAPS Police Clearance Certificate. Requirements vary by visa category and sponsor, so confirm the exact rule for your specific application with your employer, sponsor, or the relevant UAE authority.

Does the certificate need to be attested for use in the UAE?

+

Typically yes. UAE authorities generally expect foreign police certificates to be legalised before they'll accept them — for South African documents that usually means an apostille followed by attestation through the UAE embassy or consulate, and sometimes the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs once in-country. See our apostille and authentication guide for the general process, and confirm the current attestation chain required for the UAE with your sponsor or a UAE visa specialist, since requirements can change.

How recent does the certificate need to be for a UAE application?

+

UAE sponsors and authorities commonly want a recently issued certificate, but the exact validity window depends on the visa category and who is assessing it. Confirm the current requirement with your sponsor or the relevant UAE authority before submitting an older certificate.

Can I get my SAPS certificate sorted while living in New Zealand, ahead of a UAE application?

+

Yes — the SAPS certificate itself is obtained the same way regardless of destination. EM Services coordinates the SAPS application, fingerprint capture and documentation from Christchurch; the completed certificate can then go through apostille and UAE attestation as required for your specific visa.

Where can I find the official, current UAE requirement?

+

Confirm directly with your UAE employer or sponsor, the relevant Emirate's GDRFA, or the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Security (ICP) — UAE visa and attestation requirements are set by these authorities and can be updated.
